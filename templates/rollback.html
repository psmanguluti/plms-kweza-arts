How rollback works

Restoring never touches your current project file. PLMS writes the chosen version to a new, uniquely named copy (SongName_restored_v2_20261103.flp), checks it against the stored SHA-256, and leaves every saved version unchanged. You can then open the copy in FL Studio. {% if restore_dir %}New copies are saved in {{ restore_dir }}.{% endif %}
